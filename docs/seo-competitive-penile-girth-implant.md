## PAGE-LEVEL CONTENT GAPS

### Penile Girth Enhancement (EN + AR)

1. **Hero image has no alt text** (`editorial-media.ts` `girthFlagship` slot: `alt: ""`) — confirmed empty on the live production page. This is a genuine accessibility and image-SEO gap on the site's single highest-priority commercial page (`priority: 0.9`).
2. **No dedicated "candidacy" section** — unlike the implant page's `CandidateCheck` component, candidacy content on the girth page is scattered across FAQ items and a "planning pillars" section rather than a single scannable block. The competitor's suitability-check pattern (not to be copied) validates that structured candidacy content performs well for this intent.
3. **No explicit "does not increase length" statement.** The page is girth-only by design, but never states the length-vs-girth distinction outright — a query-relevant clarification competitors lead with.
4. **AR FAQ items 5 and 7 (permanence/dissolution, migration/nodules) link to English-only Insights articles**, with the link label itself apologizing in Arabic ("مقال بالإنجليزية"). This is a real EN/AR parity gap, not a hypothetical one.
5. **No numeric recovery/return-to-sex timeline on-page** (the EN Insights article `when-can-you-have-sex-after-penile-girth-enhancement` exists but isn't linked from the page's FAQ at all).
6. **Literature claim with no citation**: "Published clinical literature supports hyaluronic acid as an option for penile girth enhancement, though outcomes vary..." — a real evidence-depth gap; either cite a source or soften to avoid an unsupported claim sitting next to otherwise rigorous copy.
7. **Circumcision status is addressed only as a single FAQ answer** — thin relative to its clinical relevance and its prominence in competitor candidacy checklists.

### Penile Implant (EN + AR)

1. **No implant-specific procedure count exists** (confirmed absent from `doctor.ts`) — the authority block shows only years-of-experience/FEBU/Consultant, not a girth-page-style stat strip. This is expected (no invented numbers), but it does mean the page currently reads as less "quantified" than the girth page. Not a gap to fabricate around — an explicit note for the owner: if a real implant volume figure exists and is approved later, this is where it would go.
2. **Peyronie's disease + implant is a related-link only** — never explained on the page itself, despite being one of the clinically standard implant indications and named in the owner's brief.
3. **"Medical Trainer" claim is girth-specific but displayed unqualified on the implant page too** — `doctor.medicalTrainer.role` is literally "Medical Trainer in Penile Girth Enhancement," yet the implant page renders "Medical Trainer · AndroMax Training" without the girth-specific qualifier, which could read as a training claim about implants specifically. Worth a copy fix (display the full accurate role, or omit on this page) rather than a content gap per se.
4. **AR FAQ items 3 and 4 (sensation, recovery) link to English-only Insights articles**, same pattern as the girth page.
5. **`aboutName` in `medicalWebPageSchema` stays in English on both Arabic pages** (girth and implant) — a minor structured-data parity gap, not visible to users but relevant to the "Arabic metadata" audit item.

### Both pages

- **Hero-level AuthorityMetric "+" placement is inconsistent between locales**: EN renders "15+", AR renders "+15" for the same `yearsOfExperience` value — a formatting inconsistency, not a content gap, but worth a one-line fix for polish.
- Neither page has a "Medically reviewed by / Last reviewed" component. See dedicated section below.

## TECHNICAL SEO GAPS

1. **Girth-page hero image alt text is empty** (see above) — the only concrete technical defect found in this audit.
2. **FAQPage structured data already exists and is correctly emitted** on all four pages via the shared `<Faq>` component (auto-generates `faqPageSchema()` from the same `faqItems` array rendered visibly — confirmed live on production, 5 JSON-LD blocks per page: Person, Physician, BreadcrumbList, MedicalWebPage, FAQPage). **This is a genuine existing strength versus every competitor audited above** — none of Cleveland Clinic, NMC, Burjeel, or the aesthetic-chain group were found to use FAQ-format content at all, let alone FAQPage schema. Dr. Uría's page could not be schema-checked via the fetch tooling used in this audit.
3. Canonical and reciprocal hreflang are correct and verified live on all four pages (en-AE/ar-AE/x-default, self-referential canonical each way) — no fix needed.
4. `medicalWebPageSchema`'s `aboutName` field is hardcoded English even on Arabic pages (both girth and implant) — low-impact, worth a one-line fix.
5. No `AggregateRating` schema exists anywhere on the site today. **This audit does not recommend adding one** unless the owner later provides real, verifiable review-platform data — several aesthetic-chain competitors and Dr. Uría's page display Doctify/Google star ratings, but fabricating or estimating a rating would violate the explicit "no fake AggregateRating schema" instruction and create a factual liability.
6. Open Graph image on both pages correctly falls back to the sitewide branded share card (pre-existing R8.2.1 behavior) — not a gap.

## INTERNAL-LINK GAPS

1. **`/urologic-surgery/laparoscopic-radical-prostatectomy` does not currently link to `/penile-implant`** anywhere in its content, despite the prostatectomy page's own FAQ explicitly discussing erectile function recovery. This is the single highest-value internal-link addition identified in this audit — Dr. Molina is the only physician site found in this whole competitive set with both a live prostatectomy authority page and a live implant page, and they are not yet connected.
2. `/erectile-dysfunction` → `/penile-implant`: the implant page already links to `/erectile-dysfunction` (FAQ "alternatives" answer), but the reverse link was not confirmed in this pass — worth verifying/adding for a genuinely bidirectional ED-treatment-ladder narrative.
3. `/peyronies-disease` → `/penile-implant`: the implant page lists Peyronie's only as a `RelatedTreatments` card; confirm whether `/peyronies-disease` itself links forward to the implant page as a severe-case pathway.
4. Penile Implant has **no primary-nav or top-level entry point** — it's reachable only via the `/penile-surgery` hub and the footer. The girth page, by contrast, has a direct primary-nav item. This asymmetry is a plausible discoverability gap worth owner discussion (not proposing a nav change here — flagging for the "quick wins vs. longer-term" decision below).
5. AR footer label for the implant page (`footerServiceLinksAr`, "زراعة دعامة القضيب") is a **near-miss against the glossary's own explicit "do not use, ever" entry** (`زراعة القضيب`, logged as clinically ambiguous — reads as implantation/transplantation of the penis itself). The footer label adds "دعامة" (device) as a qualifier so it is not the literal forbidden phrase, but it diverges from the approved primary term "جراحة زراعة دعامة القضيب" used as the page H1 and from "دعامة القضيب" used in body copy — a three-way label inconsistency (footer vs. title vs. body) worth resolving to the single approved term.

## ARABIC OPPORTUNITIES

1. **زيادة محيط القضيب** — confirmed as the competitor's own primary Arabic girth term via live research. Recommend adding as a **secondary/supporting phrase** on the AR girth page (e.g., a body sentence or FAQ), explicitly **not** replacing the approved primary term "زيادة سماكة القضيب," per the owner's own instruction.
2. **فيلر القضيب** has no formal glossary entry despite "فيلر" already appearing in live AR content (the Insights article title and FAQ body text). Recommend formalizing this in the glossary rather than leaving it as an ad hoc usage — reduces future inconsistency risk.
